!Series_summary	"Comparative analysis of gene expression differences (DEGs) between the amygdalohippocampal complex  (most often the region of seizure onset in mesial temporal lobe epilepsy (mTLE)) and  neocortex from mTLE patients who undergo epilepsy surgery can be used to illuminate pathophysiological events involved in epileptogenises. Transcriptome analysis was performed on freshly resected  amygdalohippocampal and temporal lobe cortex tissue obtained after surgery of 17 patients with drug-resistant temporal lobe epilepsy from Copenhagen University Hospital."
!Series_overall_design	"Hippocampal and neocortex  samples from 17 mesial temporal lobe epilepsy patients (paired data), and neocortex from 2 patients without epilepsy results in a total of 36 samples. The tissue was surgically removed en bloc and cut to 50 mg pieces. After a short rinse in RNAlater tissue sample 1-13 was placed in Rnase-free eppendorf tubes and flash frozen in liquid nitrogen. Sample 14-17 was placed in RNAlater."

!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1	"For RNA sequencing, 200 ng input RNA was used for preparing RNA sequencing libraries, which was done using TruSeq Stranded Total RNA Library Prep Kit (Illumina) following manufacturer's instructions. Briefly, RNA was denatured and ribosomal RNA (rRNA) was removed using Ribominus Gold (Illumina). After cleanup, RNA was fragmented and 1st and 2nd strand synthesis performed. Finally, libraries was adaptor ligated and amplified. AMPure XP beads was used for cleaning up the prepared sequencing libraries and quantification was done using a Qubit fluorometer (Thermo Fisher). Sequencing libraries was paired-end sequenced (2x125 bp) on the Illumina HiSeq2500 platform. Sequenced reads was trimmed and mapped to hg19/GRCh37 reference genome using BWA-MEM v0.7.12 software."
